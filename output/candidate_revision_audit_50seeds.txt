Interpretation
  Split is the component responsible for the adaptation-lag reduction.
  Suppression is not required for the observed obsolete-persistence reduction
  and adds no statistically clear incremental improvement to that metric.
  Suppression instead reduces prototype proliferation and false stabilization,
  while slowing adaptation relative to split-only. Confidence-only reduces
  false stabilization but harms objecthood F1. Recovery-slope differences are
  inconclusive, and destabilization precision remains low.
