Targeted split threshold sweep
20 paired seeds per condition, 27 targeted configurations plus split_only.

Best adaptation-preserving region
  low threshold, evidence window 1
  lag difference vs split_only ranged from -3.800 to +2.250 steps depending on
  parent decay.
  Candidate count fell by about 11.2 to 11.6 and false stabilization fell by
  about 11.8 to 20.6 percentage points.
  Wrong-target rate did not improve and useful-candidate damage appeared when
  parent decay was enabled.

Best precision region
  medium threshold, evidence window 3
  target precision was about 94.6% to 95.0%, versus 70.2% for split_only.
  Wrong-target rate fell by about 21.6 to 21.9 percentage points.
  Adaptation lag increased by about 28.3 to 32.6 steps and target recall fell
  to about 29.9% to 31.9%.

Strict region
  medium/high thresholds with evidence window 5 produced very low churn and
  false stabilization, but adaptation lag increased by roughly 36 to 38 steps
  and obsolete-candidate hit rates collapsed.

Conclusion
  The sweep identifies a precision-plasticity frontier, not a configuration
  that simultaneously preserves split_only adaptation and improves targeting,
  false stabilization, and proliferation.
