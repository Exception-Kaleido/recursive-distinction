Interpretation
  Generate-then-select preserves the fast split adaptation and objecthood F1
  while greatly reducing candidate count and avoiding evaluator-confirmed
  useful-candidate damage. Selection corrects most of the false-stabilization
  increase caused by provisional branching alone, but does not reduce false
  stabilization below split_only. The candidate budget adds only a small count
  reduction at the tested budget of 15.
